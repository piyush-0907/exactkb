About ExactKB

ExactKB is a free tool that shrinks images and PDFs to the exact size you need, in KB or MB. It was built for the common situation where an exam form, job portal or application site rejects a file because it is a few KB too big.

What it does

You upload a JPG, PNG, WEBP or PDF, type your target size, and download a compressed copy that fits. There is no sign-up and no watermark.

Private by design

Compression runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never sent to a server, so your documents and photos stay yours. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Who made it

ExactKB is an independent project, built and maintained by a small team that cares about simple, private tools.
