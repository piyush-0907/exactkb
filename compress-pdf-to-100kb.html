Compress PDF to 100 KB

A PDF under 100 KB is possible for short, mostly-text documents. Some portals limit uploaded PDFs to around 100 KB. This is easiest to reach with a one or two page document. Check your form for the real limit.

Open the compressor →

How to compress a PDF to 100 KB

  1. Open the ExactKB tool and choose Compress PDF.
  2. Select your PDF file.
  3. Type 100 and choose KB as the target size.
  4. Compress, then download the result.

It is free, needs no account, and your file is processed in your browser, so it is never uploaded.

Tips for a good result at 100 KB

  • If the PDF is a scan, scanning at lower resolution or in grayscale makes it much lighter.
  • Remove pages you do not need before compressing.
  • Text-based PDFs, such as ones exported from Word, are already small and shrink easily.
  • Photos of pages inside a PDF are the biggest cause of large files.

What to expect

Reaching 100 KB on a multi-page scan is hard, and pages may look softer. For small targets, fewer pages gives better results. ExactKB aims just under your target so the file is accepted, and the exact size can differ slightly.

Frequently asked questions

Can I compress a PDF to 100 KB?

Often yes for short documents. A long scanned PDF may lose quite a bit of sharpness to get there.

Will my PDF still open normally?

Yes. You get a normal PDF that opens in any viewer.
