Compress PDF to 500 KB

500 KB gives you room for a multi-page scanned document with decent quality. Many portals allow up to 500 KB or 1 MB for document PDFs. Check the number on your own form.

Open the compressor →

How to compress a PDF to 500 KB

  1. Open the ExactKB tool and choose Compress PDF.
  2. Select your PDF file.
  3. Type 500 and choose KB as the target size.
  4. Compress, then download the result.

It is free, needs no account, and your file is processed in your browser, so it is never uploaded.

Tips for a good result at 500 KB

  • Combine pages into one PDF before compressing if the form wants a single file.
  • Scan at a moderate resolution. Very high settings create files that are far bigger than needed.
  • Black and white scans are much lighter than full colour ones.
  • Keep your original file so you can reuse it where a higher-quality copy is allowed.

What to expect

At 500 KB, most scanned document sets stay clear and readable. ExactKB aims just under your target so the file is accepted, and the exact size can differ slightly.

Frequently asked questions

How many pages fit in 500 KB?

It depends on content. Text pages take very little room, while scanned colour pages take much more.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no watermark on the result.
