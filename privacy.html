Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

ExactKB ("we", "the site") is a free tool that compresses images and PDFs to a size you choose. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your files stay on your device

All compression happens inside your web browser. The images and PDFs you select are not uploaded to our servers or to any third party. We cannot see, store or share your files.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or the contents of your files. The site saves one small preference in your browser's local storage: whether you chose light or dark mode. This stays on your device and you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Third-party services

To work, the page loads open-source libraries and fonts from public content delivery networks (such as Google Fonts, cdnjs and unpkg). These providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your browser requests those files, under their own privacy policies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic visit data (such as IP address) as part of operating the service. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Advertising

We may show advertisements in the future to cover the cost of running the site. If we do, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads. We will update this page if advertising or analytics are added.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
