Compress image to 200 KB

200 KB works well for scanned certificates, ID cards and mark sheets. Many sites that accept document images set a limit near 200 KB. Check the exact number on the page where you upload.

Open the compressor →

How to compress a image to 200 KB

  1. Open the ExactKB tool and choose Compress image.
  2. Select your JPG, PNG or WEBP image.
  3. Type 200 and choose KB as the target size.
  4. Compress, then download the result.

It is free, needs no account, and your file is processed in your browser, so it is never uploaded.

Tips for a good result at 200 KB

  • Use good, even lighting when you photograph a certificate. Shadows hurt both quality and size.
  • Crop to the page edges to avoid wasting size on the background.
  • If an image is several MB, compress once straight to 200 KB instead of repeatedly.
  • Keep one copy of the original in case you need a higher-quality version later.

What to expect

At 200 KB there is room for good quality, so most documents and photos look clean and legible. ExactKB aims just under your target so the file is accepted, and the exact size can differ slightly.

Frequently asked questions

Is 200 KB enough for a scanned certificate?

For a single page photo, usually yes. Text should remain easy to read.

Can I compress PNG to 200 KB?

Yes. Upload the PNG and set the target to 200 KB. The tool handles PNG, JPG and WEBP.
