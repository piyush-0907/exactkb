Compress image to 20 KB

A 20 KB limit is very small, so it usually appears on signature and small photo fields. Upload fields for signatures, thumbnails and tiny passport-style photos often ask for 20 KB or less. Check the limit printed on your own form before you start.

Open the compressor →

How to compress a image to 20 KB

  1. Open the ExactKB tool and choose Compress image.
  2. Select your JPG, PNG or WEBP image.
  3. Type 20 and choose KB as the target size.
  4. Compress, then download the result.

It is free, needs no account, and your file is processed in your browser, so it is never uploaded.

Tips for a good result at 20 KB

  • Crop the picture tightly first. Less empty space means more of the 20 KB goes to what matters.
  • Plain, light backgrounds compress far better than busy ones.
  • Use a JPG if you can. Photos saved as PNG are much heavier at the same quality.
  • For a signature, write with a dark pen on plain white paper and photograph it in good light.

What to expect

At 20 KB the tool has to shrink dimensions as well as quality, so fine detail will look soft. If the result is too blurry to read, try a tighter crop or a simpler image. ExactKB aims just under your target so the file is accepted, and the exact size can differ slightly.

Frequently asked questions

Can a photo really fit in 20 KB?

Yes, for a small or simple image. A large detailed photo will lose noticeable detail at this size.

Why is my result a little under 20 KB?

ExactKB aims just below your limit so the upload is never rejected for being a few bytes over.
