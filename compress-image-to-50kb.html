Compress image to 50 KB

50 KB is a very common limit for passport-size photos and signatures on application forms. Many online applications set a maximum of around 50 KB for a photo or signature upload. The limit differs between forms, so always read the instructions on yours.

Open the compressor →

How to compress a image to 50 KB

  1. Open the ExactKB tool and choose Compress image.
  2. Select your JPG, PNG or WEBP image.
  3. Type 50 and choose KB as the target size.
  4. Compress, then download the result.

It is free, needs no account, and your file is processed in your browser, so it is never uploaded.

Tips for a good result at 50 KB

  • Crop to the face and shoulders before compressing, as most forms expect a close-up.
  • Avoid patterned or shadowy backgrounds, which use up a lot of the size budget.
  • Photos taken on a phone can be several MB, and that is fine. The tool reduces them for you.
  • If the form also asks for particular dimensions, resize first, then compress to 50 KB.

What to expect

A clear face photo usually stays recognisable at 50 KB. Text inside the image may look slightly soft. ExactKB aims just under your target so the file is accepted, and the exact size can differ slightly.

Frequently asked questions

Is 50 KB enough for a clear passport photo?

For on-screen use, generally yes. Keep the crop tight and the background plain for the best result.

What if my file is still over 50 KB?

Crop it smaller or pick a simpler photo, then compress again. Very detailed images need more reduction.
